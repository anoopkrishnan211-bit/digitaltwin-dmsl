## SUBMISSION DOCUMENT 01: BUSINESS ANALYSIS & REQUIREMENTS

**Executive Summary**
This document demonstrates end-to-end Business Analysis capability for a hypothetical manufacturing client (DMS), framing operational transformation through structured discovery, requirements, and process analysis. The solution prioritizes visibility and governance before predictive intelligence, aligned with data-readiness realities.

**Business Context**

- **Client:** DMS Manufacturing (hypothetical).
- **Industry:** Discrete manufacturing (roll-forming lines).
- **Challenge:** Reactive maintenance, fragmented data, extended changeovers, tribal knowledge.

**Problem Statement**
Unplanned downtime, inconsistent changeover tracking, and lack of unified visibility hinder production continuity and scalability.

**Business Objectives**

- O1: Validate operational pain points and data readiness (Discovery).
- O2: Define traceable requirements (BRD, FR, NFR).
- O3: Frame Digital Twin–enabled solution at business level.
- O4: Plan governable, phased delivery.
- O5: Articulate business value without invented ROI.

**Stakeholders**

- COO (Sponsor), Plant Manager (Owner), Maintenance Lead (User), IT Manager (Integrator), Shift Supervisors (End Users).

**Current State**

- Manual logging (Excel/paper), no fault taxonomy, reactive maintenance, verbal handovers.

**Pain Points & Gaps**

- GAP-01: No unified downtime records.
- GAP-02: Changeover timing not captured.
- GAP-03: No early-warning capability.
- GAP-04: SOPs not accessible at point of decision.
- GAP-05: Inconsistent fault tagging.
- GAP-06: Shift handover context lost.

**Future State**

- Unified digital logging, enforced taxonomy, real-time dashboards, RAG SOP assistant, anomaly alerts (Phase 3+), human-in-the-loop governance.
