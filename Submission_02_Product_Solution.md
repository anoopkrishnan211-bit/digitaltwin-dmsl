## SUBMISSION DOCUMENT 02: PRODUCT & SOLUTION STRATEGY

**Product Vision**
Empower operations and maintenance teams with a unified, intelligent platform transforming reactive firefighting into proactive, data-informed decision-making.

**Product Objectives**

- PO-01 to PO-06 (see 05. Product Management).

**Capability Framework**

- CAP-01 to CAP-08 (see 05. Product Management).

**Proposed Solution**
Phased Digital Twin–enabled platform: Visibility (Phase 1) → Decision Support (Phase 2) → Predictive Intelligence (Phase 3+).

**Digital Twin Concept**
Virtual representation of lines/assets/states/events; business entity model, not engineering schema.

**Digital Thread Concept**
Continuity from physical asset → event → decision → action → outcome → learning.

**Predictive Intelligence Use Cases**

- Early-warning motor overheat alert.
- Die misalignment prediction based on cycle count.

**AI Governance Approach**

- Transparency, accountability, fairness, safety; human-in-the-loop for all actions.

**Platform Comparison**

- Low-code (Phase 1) → Industrial IoT (Phase 3+); avoid full MES initially.

**Feature Prioritization**

- Must Have: F-01, F-02, F-05, F-07 (MVP).
- Should Have: F-06, F-04, F-08 (Phase 2).
- Could Have: F-03 (Phase 3+).

**MVP Definition**

- 1–2 lines; forms, dashboards, taxonomy, audit logs; 12-week delivery.

**Product Roadmap**

- Phase 0 (4w) → Phase 1 (12w) → Phase 2 (16w) → Phase 3+ (16w+).

**KPIs & Success Metrics**

- KPI-01 to KPI-07 (see 05. Product Management, 06. Data & Information).

**Business Value**

- Improved visibility, faster decisions, reduced downtime, better planning, enhanced coordination, governed operations.
